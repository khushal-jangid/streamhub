COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you install

Do downloaded movies fill up my phone gallery or public Downloads folder?

No. StreamHub stores offline video inside the private Android application directory alongside a media exclusion file, keeping your gallery clean and deleting cached downloads automatically when you uninstall.

Will downloads stop if I close the app or lock my phone screen?

No. Android builds run a dedicated foreground sync service with CPU and network locks, showing live megabyte progress in your notification drawer until the download finishes.

How does StreamHub achieve higher download speeds for 4K files?

Whenever upstream servers support HTTP byte ranges, StreamHub splits the file across up to ten parallel worker streams and tracks completed segments on disk so pause and resume remain lossless.

Why does Android or Windows SmartScreen ask permission the first time?

StreamHub ships outside commercial app stores as direct open software. You can verify the exact SHA256 digest shown above against your downloaded file before opening it.

How do future app updates work after installation?

On every app launch, StreamHub checks this repository manifest and displays an update popup whenever a newer release or instant update patch is published.

Is any account registration or subscription fee required?

No account or payment is required. All profile names, preferred audio languages, themes, and watch history stay locally on your device.

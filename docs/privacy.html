Privacy Policy

StreamHub runs locally on your Android device or Windows computer. No telemetry, watch history, or personal profile data leaves your device.

Local app storage

Preferences, playback progress, and offline movie downloads live exclusively inside your private application directory. Offline video files are marked with a media exclusion file so your device gallery remains clean and untouched, and all cached downloads remove cleanly upon uninstall.

External network requests

The app fetches catalog feeds, video stream links, subtitles, and public GitHub release manifests directly from your device on request. Release packages verify checksums locally before installation.
